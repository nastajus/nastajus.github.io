Job Description

## Job Posting 2: AI Trainer / AI Instructor

Job Description:

• Design and deliver training programs on Artificial Intelligence, Machine
Learning, and related technologies.

• Create instructional materials, including presentations, manuals, and hands-
on exercises.

• Conduct workshops, seminars, and online sessions for learners at various
levels.

• Guide learners through practical AI projects and real-world applications.

• Evaluate learner performance and provide constructive feedback for
improvement.

• Stay updated on emerging AI tools, techniques, and industry trends.

• Collaborate with curriculum designers and technical teams to enhance
training content.

• Ensure learning objectives are met and participants gain practical AI
skills.

* * *

Job Requirements:

• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, AI, Data Science, or a
related field.

• Strong knowledge of AI, machine learning, and neural networks.

• Experience in teaching, training, or mentoring in a technical environment.

• Proficiency in Python, TensorFlow, PyTorch, or other AI frameworks.

• Excellent communication, presentation, and instructional skills in English.

• Ability to simplify complex concepts for diverse learners.

• Strong organizational skills and ability to manage multiple training
sessions.

• Experience with online teaching platforms and tools is an asset.
