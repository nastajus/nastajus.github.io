Job Title: Assistant Receptionist

Department: Administrative & Legal: Clerical/Administrative Support/Reception/Legal/Paralegal/ Legal Assistant

Hours of Work: Full Time

Job Number: RIW608-16007

Main Intersection: Bathurst St. & King St. W

Job Description

Client Service & Front Desk

Greet and welcome clients with a warm, professional attitude.

  • Check clients in and out and ensure a smooth, positive experience.
  • Answer incoming phone calls and respond to client questions.
  • Schedule, confirm, reschedule, and manage client appointments.
  • Manage the salon booking system and maintain an organized appointment calendar.
  • Handle cancellations, late arrivals, and appointment changes professionally.
  • Assist clients with selecting services and answer questions about salon offerings.
  • Process payments and accurately handle transactions.
  • Maintain a professional and inviting front desk and reception area.
  • Build positive relationships with clients and encourage repeat visits.

Salon Cleanliness & Organization

Keep the front desk, waiting area, retail area, and common spaces clean, tidy, and organized.

  • Regularly wipe down and sanitize high-touch surfaces.
  • Help maintain overall salon cleanliness throughout the day.
  • Ensure stations and client areas are neat and presentable.
  • Empty garbage and recycling as needed.
  • Restock front desk, washroom, and salon supplies.

Coffee & Client Hospitality

Prepare and offer coffee, tea, and water to clients when appropriate.

  • Keep the coffee/hospitality area clean, stocked, and organized.
  • Ensure clients feel comfortable and well cared for during their visit.

Laundry & Salon Support

Collect, sort, wash, dry, fold, and organize salon towels and linens.

  • Keep clean towels and linens properly stocked and readily available.
  • Maintain laundry areas and equipment in a clean and organized condition.
  • Assist the salon team with day-to-day tasks as needed.

Team Support & Daily Operations

Communicate effectively with stylists and management regarding appointments, client needs, and salon operations.

  • Help open and close the salon according to established procedures.
  • Assist with stocking products and supplies.
  • Monitor inventory of front desk, cleaning, coffee, laundry, and salon supplies and notify management when items need to be reordered.
  • Support promotional activities, retail sales, and client outreach when required.
  • Maintain a positive, professional, and team-oriented environment.
  • Perform other reasonable duties required to support the smooth operation of the salon.

Qualifications & Skills

Previous customer service, reception, hospitality, or salon experience is an asset.

  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Friendly, welcoming and professional personality.
  • Strong organizational and time-management skills.
  • Ability to multitask in a busy salon environment.
  • Comfortable answering phones and working with appointment-booking software.
  • Reliable, punctual, and detail-oriented.
  • Able to maintain a clean and organized environment.
  • Ability to work independently while also being a supportive team member.
  • Professional appearance and a positive attitude.
