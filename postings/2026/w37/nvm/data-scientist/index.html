Job Title: Data Scientist

Department: Technology: Information Technology/Telecommunications

Hours of Work:

Job Number: MOJ512-15343

Main Intersection: Yonge & Steels

Job Description

ob Description:

• Collect, clean, and analyze structured and unstructured data to support
business insights and decision-making.

• Develop predictive models, machine learning algorithms, and data-driven
solutions.

• Visualize data trends and patterns using charts, dashboards, and reports.

• Collaborate with cross-functional teams including engineering, marketing,
and product development to implement data solutions.

• Conduct statistical analyses to identify trends, anomalies, and
opportunities.

• Communicate findings and recommendations clearly to technical and non-
technical stakeholders.

• Maintain documentation for data models, pipelines, and analysis processes.

• Stay current with data science methodologies, tools, and industry best
practices.

* * *

Job Requirements:

• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics,
Data Science, or a related field.

• Proven experience in data analysis, statistical modeling, or machine
learning.

• Proficiency in programming languages such as Python, R, or SQL.

• Experience with data visualization tools (e.g., Tableau, Power BI,
Matplotlib, Seaborn).

• Strong analytical, problem-solving, and critical-thinking skills.

• Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English.

• Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a fast-paced
environment.

• Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP) and big data tools is an
asset.
