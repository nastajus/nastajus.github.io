Job Title: Graphic Designer & Illustrator

Department: Media: Advertising/Marketing/Public Relations/ Media/Journalism/Newspaper

Hours of Work: Full Time

Job Number: ANI603-15465

Main Intersection: 333 North Rivermede Road, Unit 5, Concord, Ontario, L4K 3N7

Job Description

Graphic Designer & Illustrator – Immediate, Permanent, Full- Time On-Site
Role – $55,000- 60.000.

Start Date: Immediate

Overview

Link Product Solutions Limited is one of Canada’s fastest growing and leading
suppliers of Seasonal, Stationery and General Merchandise products to
independent and major retailers in North America, the Caribbean, Central, and
South America. Located in Vaughan, Ont. we operate in a 150,000 sq. ft Office,
Distribution and Showroom facility with offsite satellite warehouses to
service our retailers’ efficiently.

Currently, we are recruiting an experienced Graphic Designer/Illustrator with
a proven track record in Creative Graphic Designs, specifically with Consumer
Packaging and Production background.

Job Summary: This role will appeal to a results-oriented, Creative Graphic
Designer specifically in Product Packaging Design. Proficient both with
digital and traditional artwork, print and packaging design; and who enjoys
significant challenge in a fast paced, entrepreneurial environment.

Benefits of joining us:

* Comprehensive Company Paid Benefits – Single/Family – Health/Dental/Vision/AD/D & Life Insurance
* Permanent Full Time – Immediate position – On-Site
* Monday – Friday – 9.00 a.m. – 5.00 p.m. = 35 hours weekly.
* An opportunity to grow professionally long-term in a multicultural, diverse, environment which highly values family, exchange of ideas with a supportive and positive team

Key Job Responsibilities:

* Creating creative custom graphic designs/Illustrations for product packaging and production ensuring brand consistency
* Creating and updating catalogues, flyers and other marketing materials
* Supporting the sales department to create and manage designs from concept through to final deliverables
* Working closely with the graphic team to develop branding materials, new design concepts, Illustrations, graphics and layouts
* Designing, illustrating, size, color, interpret/understand color palettes
* Creating and developing artwork (digital, illustrative, typography)
* Ensure graphics are properly executed from creative concept to production in accordance with company quality standards
* Present graphics at various stages of the design process for review
* Researching market trends for new graphic concepts and sharing ideas with the team
* Photography and editing of sample products as required

Job Requirements:

* Graphic Design Diploma with 3+ years recent related Graphic Designer/ Illustrator in Packaging Designs
* Portfolio Required
* Experienced in Creative Illustration with excellent knowledge of Adobe Creative Suite (Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign)
* Strong understanding of the CPG industry, branding and product development/design
* Strong conceptual and creative design skills
* Extensive knowledge of Adobe Creative Suite (Illustrator, Photoshop & InDesign).
* Photography and editing skills
* Understand the vision of the brand and product direction
* Effective communication of ideas and key information to the team
* Self-confident and resilient to constructive criticism on feedback regarding design work
* Time management and organizational skills to manage priorities and workflow
* Ability to work independently and strong leadership skills
* Work efficiently within tight deadlines for graphic creation
* Must have great attention to detail eye for typography and kerning
* Flexible strong written and verbal communication skills (English)
* Ability to work in a fast-paced, deadline-driven team environment with multiple projects

This is an excellent opportunity for a Graphic Designer & Creative Illustrator
experienced in packaging design and who is passionate and eager to contribute
innovative ideas to our projects.

We thank all applicants, however, only candidates considered for an interview
will be contacted. Please visit our website at
[www.linkproducts.ca](http://www.linkproducts.ca/)
