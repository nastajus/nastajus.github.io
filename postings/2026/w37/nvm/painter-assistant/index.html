Job Description

ob Title: Painter Assistant – Residential Painting

Job Description:

• Assist with residential interior and exterior painting projects, ensuring
high-quality results in clients’ homes.

• Prepare surfaces by sanding, scraping, filling holes, caulking, cleaning
walls, and applying primer as needed.

• Help mix and match paint colors and apply paints using brushes, rollers, or
other tools under the lead painter’s direction.

• Set up and protect work areas using drop sheets, tape, coverings, and proper
masking techniques.

• Move furniture, cover household items, and ensure the client’s home is kept
clean and protected during work.

• Maintain tools, clean brushes/rollers, and organize materials at the
beginning and end of each workday.

• Provide excellent customer service by being respectful, courteous, and
maintaining a professional demeanor in clients’ homes.

* * *

Job Requirements:

• Basic conversational English to follow instructions and communicate with
clients and team members.

• 1–2 years of residential painting or general home renovation experience
(Canada or internationally).

• Ability to perform physical tasks, including lifting paint cans, climbing
ladders, and standing for extended periods.

• Familiarity with basic painting tools and techniques, including cutting-in,
rolling, taping, and prepping surfaces.

• Strong attention to detail with a focus on clean, neat workmanship.

• Reliable, punctual, and able to work independently or as part of a team.

• Willingness to learn new techniques and grow within the residential painting
trade.
