Job Description

Specifications Writing Assistant (Architecture) – Entry Level

Location: Remote

Job description: A boutique firm specializing in high-quality construction
specifications. We work with leading architectural practices and manufacturers
to develop clear, accurate, and cost-effective documentation for building
projects, is seeking an entry-level Specifications Writing Assistant to join
our growing team. The role is ideal for someone with a background or strong
interest in architecture or construction who enjoys technical writing and
structured problem-solving. We offer flexible hours and remote work
arrangements.

Responsibilities:

– Assist in preparing project specifications and updating technical
documents, including master specifications, product databases, marketing
materials, and internal resources.

– Coordinate with project managers, consultants, clients, contractors, and
suppliers to ensure the accuracy and quality of documentation.

– Research construction materials and systems, including attending supplier
meetings and product presentations.

– Develop and maintain organized digital filing systems.

Requirements:

– Diploma or degree in architecture, architectural technology, engineering,
construction management, or a related discipline.

– Graduates in technical writing or communications will be considered if they
demonstrate a solid understanding of construction or design principles.

– Excellent written and spoken English. A writing sample is required.

– Proficiency with Microsoft Office.

– Strong organizational and time management skills.

Assets

– Ability to read and write in French.
