Job Title: Graphic Designer / Office Administrator

Department: Administrative & Legal: Clerical/Administrative Support/Reception/Legal/Paralegal/ Legal Assistant

Hours of Work:

Job Number: DHC606-15793

Main Intersection: King St West at Dunn Ave

Job Description

Position Overview

We are hiring a Graphic Designer – Office Administrator for a part-
time role with the clear goal of transitioning to full-time based on
performance and company growth.

This is a hybrid role combining creative design work with essential office
administration. You will play a key part in both the creative production and
the smooth day-to-day operations of our growing business.

Key Responsibilities

# Graphic Design & Production

* Design and prepare print-ready files for:
* Business Cards
* Flyers
* Brochures
* Letters & Labels
* Posters
* Menus
* Signs & Banners
* Manage revisions and ensure all work meets high-quality standards
* Support print production workflows

# Office Administration

* Handle office organization and file/record keeping
* Manage inbound/outbound emails and telephone communications
* Prepare quotations for clients and suppliers
* Schedule work, manage time, and expedite jobs
* Track financial records and maintain accurate documentation
* Provide continuous follow-up with clients and vendors
* Support overall distribution and communication needs

Qualifications & Requirements

Must Have:

* Personable, sociable personality with excellent communication skills
* Strong attention to detail and a commitment to absolute accuracy and uncompromising quality
* Ability to work independently in a fast-paced, multitasking environment
* Comfortable managing multiple priorities and deadlines

Assets / Preferred:

* Experience with Adobe Creative Suite (Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign)
* Previous experience in print production
* Own a personal laptop (Mac or PC)

What We Offer

* Part-time hours to start, with strong potential to grow into a full-time position
* Opportunity to be part of a growing local business
* Hands-on role with variety — no two days are the same
* Collaborative, community-focused work environment
