Job Title: Assistant Teacher – Level 3

Location: Richmond Hill, ON; High Park, Toronto, ON; Riverdale, Toronto, ON; Willowdale, Toronto, ON

Employment Type: Part-Time

Salary: $26.00 per hour

About the Role

An established education organization is seeking Level 3 Assistant Teachers to provide instructional and classroom support across its Richmond Hill, High Park, Riverdale, and Willowdale locations. This role provides more active classroom responsibilities than a traditional Assistant Teacher and offers opportunities to lead activities, support students, and independently teach or substitute teach when required.

The successful candidate will have strong communication and classroom management skills and be committed to supporting student learning and development. This position is designed as a developmental pathway toward becoming a Primary Teacher, providing hands-on classroom experience and opportunities for performance-based progression.

Key Responsibilities

  • Support lesson delivery alongside the Primary Teacher and lead pre-assigned classroom activities, group work, drills, and core topics
  • Provide targeted academic support to new and struggling students and assist with homework sessions when assigned
  • Support assessments and work independently with new students or students requiring additional assistance
  • Independently teach or provide substitute teaching coverage in the absence of the Primary Teacher
  • Maintain classroom flow, student engagement, and behavioural expectations while supporting the Primary Teacher
  • Assist with classroom setup, transitions, attendance tracking, and maintaining classroom standards
  • Assist with marking, lesson preparation, and other academic activities as assigned
  • Provide observational feedback on student progress to the Primary Teacher
  • Assist with updating student records and Brightspace information as required

Key Skills & Experience

  • Must be 18 years of age or older
  • Bachelor's degree required
  • Successful completion of mandatory vulnerability/background screening required
  • Completion of internal Math and Teacher training, including 60 hours of paid mandatory training
  • Ability to commit to regular weekly shifts
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills
  • Professional classroom conduct and ability to maintain positive student relationships
  • Strong organizational skills and ability to follow instructional procedures and classroom standards
  • Ability to support students with different learning needs and work independently when required
